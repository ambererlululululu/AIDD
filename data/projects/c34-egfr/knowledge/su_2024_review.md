# Su et al. 2024 — 4th-Gen EGFR-TKI Review (Layer 1 Summary)

**Full title**: Fourth-generation epidermal growth factor receptor-tyrosine kinases inhibitors: hope and challenges
**Journal**: Transl. Cancer Res. 2024
**DOI**: 10.21037/tcr-24-406
**PMC**: PMC11384918

## Classification of 4th-Gen Approaches

### 1. ATP-Competitive (Orthosteric) — Majority of compounds
- Reversible binding in ATP pocket
- **BBT-176**, **BLU-945**, **TQB-3804**, **BI-4020**, **JND3229**, **H002**, **JIN-A02**

### 2. Allosteric
- Bind outside ATP pocket, target activation domain
- **EAI045**, **JBJ-04-125-02**, **JBJ-09-063**
- Limitation: Require anti-EGFR antibody (cetuximab) for efficacy

### 3. Irreversible (Non-C797 Targeting)
- **BDTX-1535**: Irreversible but targets different residue/mechanism
- Broadest mutation coverage among all candidates

## Key Compounds — Clinical Status & Data

### BBT-176 (Terminated)
- Reversible, non-covalent, sulfone-Lys745 interaction
- Potent nanomolar activity vs Del19/T790M/C797S and L858R/C797S
- IC50 reduced 50x when combined with cetuximab
- Phase I/II (NCT04820023): **Terminated**. Successor BBT-207 in development
- Only 1/18 patients achieved PR

### BLU-945 (Discontinued)
- Reversible, WT-sparing
- Cannot inhibit 19del/C797S without T790M co-mutation
- SYMPHONY trial: Limited single-agent efficacy → discontinued

### TQB-3804 (Phase I/II, No Data Released)
- Targets T790M/C797S, L858R/T790M/C797S, L858R/T790M
- IC50: 0.218 (L858R/T790M/C797S), 7.92 (WT) → 36x selectivity
- Phase I (NCT04128085), Phase II (NCT04180150) — no clinical data yet

### BDTX-1535 (Most Promising)
- **Broadest coverage**: C797S + L858R + Ex19del + L718Q + L747P + E709V
- Irreversible mechanism (unlike most 4th-gen)
- **Excellent brain penetration**
- Phase I (NCT05256290): 50% ORR (6/12 patients), 100% DCR
- Potential first-line use (not just post-resistance)

### BI-4020 (Preclinical)
- Aminobenzimidazole macrocycle — "Start Selective and Rigidify"
- 10 mg/kg: Strong regression in all 10 tumor models
- IC50 = 0.20 nM (BaF3 Del19/T790M/C797S)
- TGI = 121% in PC9 xenograft

### EAI045 / JBJ Series (Stalled)
- Allosteric — inherent selectivity
- EAI045 requires cetuximab combination
- No clinical trials initiated despite discovery in 2016/2017

## Key Challenges

1. **Small target population**: C797S only in 5-7% of 1st-line osimertinib patients
2. **Osimertinib competition**: Combinations (FLAURA-2, MARIPOSA) raising the bar
3. **Slow development**: Most compounds still preclinical after years
4. **Lack of proper cell models**: Most use engineered Ba/F3, not NSCLC-derived C797S lines
5. **Complex resistance**: Non-EGFR mechanisms post-osimertinib are unaddressed
6. **Genetic testing burden**: Requires mutation confirmation before treatment

## SAR Principles Across Compounds

1. **Reversible binding** is dominant (C797S eliminates covalent target)
2. **WT sparing** achieved via hydrophobic gatekeeper interactions (MET790)
3. **Hinge region H-bonding** (MET793) is universal requirement
4. **CNS penetration** increasingly important (BDTX-1535, H002, THE-349)
5. **Combination therapy** needed for allosteric inhibitors
6. **Broader mutation coverage** = competitive advantage
