# Eno et al. 2022 — BLU-945 (Layer 1 Summary)

**Full title**: Discovery of BLU-945, a Reversible, Potent, and Wild-Type-Sparing Next-Generation EGFR Mutant Inhibitor for Treatment-Resistant Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer
**Journal**: J. Med. Chem. 2022, 65, 9662-9677
**DOI**: 10.1021/acs.jmedchem.2c00704
**Group**: Blueprint Medicines

## Design Strategy

BLU-945 was designed as a reversible, non-covalent inhibitor that fits the ATP binding pocket of EGFR even with C797S mutation, while sparing wild-type EGFR. The scaffold adopts a conformation incompatible with WT EGFR active site geometry.

## Key SAR Findings

### Piperidinol Modifications
- Most modifications to piperidinol ring → reduced activity
- **Fluorine next to alcohol**: 2-fold potency improvement without losing WT selectivity
- Piperidinol provides critical H-bond donor/acceptor

### Cyclization Strategy (Key Breakthrough)
- Cyclization through amide carbonyl + aminopyridine NH → **2,7-naphthyridine**
- Mimics intramolecular hydrogen bond of acyclic precursor
- **Compound 6** (cyclized): Significant increase in biochemical AND cellular potency
- Compound 6 maintained WT selectivity — cyclization does not compromise selectivity

### Crystal Structure
- X-ray of compound 7 in ATP pocket: PDB 8D73
- Bound to EGFR L858R/T790M protein

### Selectivity Profiling
- KINOMEscan selectivity profiling of compound 6 analogs
- Confirmed narrow kinase selectivity

## Target Profile

| Property | Data |
|----------|------|
| Target mutations | T790M/C797S, T790M, L858R/T790M |
| Mechanism | Reversible, non-covalent ATP-competitive |
| WT sparing | Yes — designed for selectivity |
| Limitation | Cannot effectively inhibit 19del/C797S (without T790M) |

## Clinical Development

| Phase | Trial | Status |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Phase I/II | SYMPHONY (NCT04862780) | **Discontinued** |
| Combo | BLU-945 + osimertinib | Showed better response than monotherapy |

### Clinical Observations
- Higher doses associated with broad EGFR mutation coverage including L858R ± C797S
- Generally well-tolerated, no significant WT-EGFR-associated adverse events
- Limited efficacy as single agent in subsequent-line population
- Development has been terminated

## Relevance to C34 Project

BLU-945 validates the reversible non-covalent approach for C797S targeting. Key lessons:
1. **Cyclization** can dramatically improve potency while maintaining selectivity
2. **WT sparing** is achievable through scaffold design
3. **Monotherapy** may be insufficient — combination strategies needed
4. BLU-945's failure in clinic highlights need for broader mutation coverage (C34 should demonstrate Del19 activity)
5. Different scaffold (2,7-naphthyridine vs thiazole-pyrimidine) but same binding pocket
